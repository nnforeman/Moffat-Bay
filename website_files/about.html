About Moffat Bay Lodge

Moffat Bay Lodge is located on Joviedsa Island in Washington State's San Juan Islands. Surrounded by beautiful coastal scenery and the natural beauty of the Pacific Northwest, the lodge offers guests a relaxing island getaway. Whether you want to unwind, explore the outdoors, or enjoy local wildlife, Moffat Bay Lodge is a welcoming place to relax and reconnect with nature.
