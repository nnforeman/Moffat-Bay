Cedar Hollow Lodge

Tucked beneath old-growth cedars just a short walk from the pool, this lodge blends rustic timber charm with modern comfort. Wood-burning fireplaces, wraparound porches, and a view of the strait make this the island's coziest retreat.

Whistling Strait Inn

Perched on the bluffs overlooking Joviedsa's windswept northern coast, this inn is known for its floor-to-ceiling windows and front-row seats to passing orcas. Perfect for early risers who don't mind a stiff sea breeze.

Fembrook Retreat

Surrounded by moss-draped forest trails and a quiet freshwater creek, Fembrook is the island's go-to for hikers and hammock campers alike. Each cabin comes with its own outdoor cooking tub.

Harbor Light Lodge

Set right in Joviedsa's small fishing village, this lodge puts guests steps from the docks, local seafood shacks, and the weekly farmers market. Rooms feature nautical decor and views of the working harbor.

Ravenwood Lodge

A dark, moodier option nestled deep in the island's interior forest, Ravenwood leans into rustic-luxury with stone fireplaces, exposed timber beams, and a reputation for spotting the resident raven flock at dusk.

Tidepool Bluff Cottages

A cluster of small, private cottages along the southern shoreline, each with a private path down to tide pools teeming with starfish and anemones. Popular with families and low-key beach lovers.
